    How to read this
    NO EXPERIENCE NEEDED · FULL DEFINITIONS: THESTRAT.AI/DOCS ↗
    
    
    Every market makes one bar per period: a day, a week, a month, a quarter, a year. We ask only one question: did price break last period's high, its low, both, or neither? That is TheStrat. No opinions. No news. Just what price did.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Stocks bought · bonds soldRisk-on. Money leaves safety for growth. Yields rise.
    
      
    Stocks sold · bonds boughtFlight to safety. Yields fall.
    
      
    Stocks sold · bonds soldCapital leaving the country. Yields rise. The strongest SELL.
    
      
    Stocks bought · bonds boughtLiquidity wave. Yields fall.
    
    
    
    
    Bonds are read through government-bond fund prices. A bond's price and its yield move in opposite directions, and a price only falls when sellers accept lower bids: bond prices falling = that country's debt is being sold = its yields rise. Every trade has a buyer and a seller, so "money flow" here means which side is pushing price, not money that physically moves. Some markets use US-listed funds priced in dollars (Chile, Poland, Norway, Thailand, Philippines, Saudi Arabia, China bonds, emerging-market bonds), so their moves include the currency. "All 5 timeframes green" means the day, week, month, quarter and year bars all trade above where they opened. Bars still trading are live and can change until they close.
