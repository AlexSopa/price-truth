    How to read this
    NO EXPERIENCE NEEDED
    
    
    Every market makes one bar per period: a day, a week, a month, a quarter, a year. We ask only one question: did price break last period's high, its low, both, or neither? That is TheStrat. No opinions. No news. Just what price did.
    
    
    
    
    Bonds are read through government-bond fund prices. Bond prices falling = people selling that country's debt (its interest rates going up). "Full continuity" means the day, week, month, quarter and year all point the same way.
